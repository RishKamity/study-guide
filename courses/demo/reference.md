# ECON 101 Midterm 1: formula and concept sheet (demo)

## Supply and demand
| Idea | Rule |
|---|---|
| Law of demand | Price up, quantity demanded down |
| Law of supply | Price up, quantity supplied up |
| Equilibrium | Qd = Qs |
| Demand shift | P and Q move the same way |
| Supply shift | P and Q move opposite ways |

## Costs
| Measure | Formula |
|---|---|
| Total cost | TC = FC + VC |
| Average total cost | ATC = TC ÷ Q |
| Average variable cost | AVC = VC ÷ Q |
| Marginal cost | MC = ΔTC for one more unit |

## Market structure
- Perfect competition: P = MR = MC; zero long-run profit.
- Monopoly: MR = MC, P from demand, P > MC; deadweight loss.
